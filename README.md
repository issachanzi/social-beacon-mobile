# Social Beacon

Social Beacon is an app for irl connections with irl friends, right now.

If you have some free time at short notice, it's not fun to individually text friends 
who are likely to be busy, just in case they might be free to catch up.

Instead, Social Beacon lets you notify all your friends at once, and they can respond 
only if they are free, or just ignore otherwise. No awkward "Sorry I'm busy tonight" 
messages, no pressure to be available.
